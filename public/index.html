🚀 Automated Large Dataset Ingestion (REST API)

Batch Pipeline Ready

When integrating with a large voice dataset (e.g. Common Voice, Hugging Face, or bulk studio WAV files), use these script templates to programmatically ingest thousands of audio files with automatic metadata tagging. 

import requests
import base64
import os

API_ENDPOINT = "https://your-applet-url.run.app/api/upload-audio"

def ingest_voice_sample(filepath, target_filename, dialect="asante_twi", phrase_id="confirm_twi"):
    with open(filepath, "rb") as audio_file:
        encoded_audio = base64.b64encode(audio_file.read()).decode("utf-8")

    payload = {
        "filename": target_filename,
        "base64Data": f"data:audio/mp3;base64,{encoded_audio}",
        "phraseId": phrase_id,
        "dialect": dialect,
        "quality": "studio_master"
    }

    res = requests.post(API_ENDPOINT, json=payload)
    print(f"Ingested {target_filename}: {res.status_code} - {res.json()}")

🛡️ The Zero-PIN Voice Security Gate

Security Pillar

Standard MoMo users in Ghana are vulnerable to voice fraud and eavesdropping. Ɔkwankyerɛfo Pa enforces a strict Zero-PIN Voice Rule:

🚫
No PIN Ever Keyed over Phone Line

The system never asks the caller to enter their 4-digit PIN over DTMF or speak it into the phone. DTMF tones can be intercepted and recorded.

🗣️
Safe Read-Back Before Any Action

The system reads back the verified real name of the recipient (not just numbers) and the exact amount in the caller's chosen language.

📲
Secure Out-of-Band Telco Screen Prompt

Upon voice confirmation (Key 1), the call gracefully ends and instructs the user: "Check your screen now to enter your MoMo PIN." PIN entry remains private on the user's encrypted SIM/USSD channel.

🔍 Why a Call Might Say "Not Reachable" & How to Fix It

Your Render server and VoiceXML gateway are verified 100% active and healthy. If dialing +233 30 804 8098 says "not reachable" on your phone, check these 3 carrier items:

  1. Correct Local Dialing Format:
    When calling from Ghana on MTN, Telecel, or AirtelTigo, try dialing with the local trunk prefix: 030 804 8098 as well as +233 30 804 8098.
  2. Africa's Talking Account Balance:
    In Africa's Talking, receiving inbound voice calls on virtual numbers requires an active prepaid wallet balance (even $1.00 or GHS 10.00). If your AT account balance is $0.00, Africa's Talking's telecom carrier automatically drops incoming calls before they reach your webhook. Check your balance in the top-right of the AT dashboard.
  3. Check AT Voice Logs:
    In africastalking.com → Voice → Call Logs / Sessions: if you see your call listed, check the status (e.g. Insufficient Balance or Callback Error). If no call is listed at all, the telecom provider hasn't finished routing the number to your account.

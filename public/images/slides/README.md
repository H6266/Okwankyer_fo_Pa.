# Hero Slider Photographic Assets Directory

This directory contains the visual assets loaded by the hero slider on the landing page (`src/config/slides.ts`).

## Image Slots & Requirements

1. **`slide_market_trader.svg` / `.jpg`**
   - **Subject**: Confident, dignified Ghanaian market woman trader at an open-air market (e.g. Makola or Kejetia) holding a mobile phone. Portrayed as competent, tech-capable, and handling transactions with ease.
   - **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 aspect ratio)
   - **Focal Point**: 50% 40%

2. **`slide_rural_farmer.svg` / `.jpg`**
   - **Subject**: Rural cocoa farmer resting or standing in a farm setting, using a classic button/feature phone to make a voice call.
   - **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 aspect ratio)
   - **Focal Point**: 50% 50%

3. **`slide_visually_impaired.svg` / `.jpg`**
   - **Subject**: Visually impaired Ghanaian university student using headphones or holding a phone close while navigating with white cane in background. Portrayed as independent, empowered, and in control of their finances.
   - **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 aspect ratio)
   - **Focal Point**: 50% 45%

4. **`slide_elderly_pensioner.svg` / `.jpg`**
   - **Subject**: An elderly Ghanaian elder/pensioner smiling warmly at home or on a veranda, holding a phone calmly without anxiety.
   - **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 aspect ratio)
   - **Focal Point**: 50% 35%

5. **`slide_security_zero_pin.svg` / `.jpg`**
   - **Subject**: Detailed shot of a mobile phone displaying the private USSD RequestToPay push ("Enter MoMo PIN to authorize GHS 500 to Kwame Nyamebere") with audio waveforms indicating the voice line is safely muted.
   - **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 aspect ratio)
   - **Focal Point**: 50% 50%
